**It does:**
- run your strategy exactly like paper;
- turn each trade into a signed, numbered, expiring signal for your executor, inside your limits;
- keep the signal until it is delivered;
- record what your executor reports;
- pause new buys when your executor is silent or a loss limit trips;
- tell you each time.

**It never:**
- holds, imports or asks for a key or a seed phrase;
- signs or sends a transaction, or builds one;
- lets a model, a tool or a chat tap arm real mode, change a limit, lift the kill switch or reset the breaker;
- trades a test signal;
- promises a fill or a profit.

Your executor decides, at its own price, with its own fees.
