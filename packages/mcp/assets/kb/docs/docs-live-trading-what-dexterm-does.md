- **Finds and explains.** The screener, the token screen, the LARP check (https://dexterm.xyz/docs/larp) and the analyst (https://dexterm.xyz/docs/analyst) read public data. None of them can trade.
- **Practises on paper.** The paper ticket, paper accounts (https://dexterm.xyz/docs/autotrade#paper-accounts) and paper autotrade runs (the yolo preset (https://dexterm.xyz/docs/yolo) included) fill on a simulated book on your machine.
- **Tells you in Telegram.** With Telegram (https://dexterm.xyz/docs/telegram) set up and paired, a paper run sends candidates, entries, exits, halts and a daily summary to your chat, and takes `/status`, `/pause`, `/resume`, `/kill`, `/flatten` and `/use` from it.
