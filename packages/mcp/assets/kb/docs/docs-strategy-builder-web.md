Your strategies, the builder, the strategy factory and the chat in one local web page, plus a Watch it cook button that opens a read-only dexterm window on one strategy's paper run with the strategy overlay. It runs on your own machine only. Paper only; not financial advice.

`dexterm autotrade web` opens the Strategy Builder in your browser. It is the same strategies, factory and chat you have in the terminal, laid out on one page, served from your own machine. Nothing in it is hosted anywhere, and it loads nothing from the internet.

**Read this first.** Everything here is paper and simulated. A strategy is a set of rules, not an edge. The factory's generated candidates are tagged GENERATED, and most of them fail their tests. dexterm never holds a key and never signs or sends a transaction, and nothing on this page can change that.
