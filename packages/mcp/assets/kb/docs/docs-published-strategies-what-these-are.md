dexterm ships the paper strategies its authors tested in November 2026 as autotrade (https://dexterm.xyz/docs/autotrade) presets, so you can read, copy, replay and forward-test the exact rules yourself. They are published so you can test them, not because they work.

> **PAPER · SIMULATED · NOT TYPICAL · NOT FINANCIAL ADVICE.** No strategy here has shown an edge. Most entries lose. Past paper results do not repeat, and real fills on thin pools can be worse than the simulated ones.

Three come from a strategy search (S2, S3, T2), three are their **open** versions (the same rules with the holder-safety and pool-safety gates switched off, which exist to show what those filters are worth), and two are the strategies of the authors' 30 SOL stream challenge.
