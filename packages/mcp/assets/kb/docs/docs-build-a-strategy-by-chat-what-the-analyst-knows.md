The analyst's tools for this: `strategy_journey` (the ladder for one strategy), `strategy_backtest` (with the held-out split), `strategy_forward_start`, `strategy_forward_stop`, `strategy_forward_report` and `strategy_promote_propose`. It is told the whole ladder, refuses to skip a rung, and reports numbers with their trade counts and the verdict lines as they are. An observe-only scan session gets none of the starting, stopping or proposing tools.

Paper by default; experimental; not financial advice.
